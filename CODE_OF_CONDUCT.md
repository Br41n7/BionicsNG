# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

BionicsNG is intended to be a collaborative space for open research and engineering. We aim to make participation respectful and constructive regardless of professional background, experience level, nationality, disability, gender, identity, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

- Treat other contributors with respect.
- Discuss evidence, assumptions, methods, and technical decisions in good faith.
- Welcome corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Give appropriate credit and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Keep criticism focused on work rather than personal attacks.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and the limits of others' time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without consent.
- Deliberate fabrication of evidence, citations, credentials, test results, or affiliations.
- Intimidation, persistent disruption, or knowingly misrepresenting another contributor's work.
- Encouraging unsafe human testing or presenting unvalidated devices as clinically proven.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed on the repository profile, or open an issue only if it is safe and appropriate to do so. Do not post private or sensitive details publicly. Maintainers should review reports fairly, keep them confidential where reasonably possible, and take proportionate action.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, maintainers may issue a reminder, request changes, temporarily restrict participation, or remove content or access. Contributors may ask for a review of an enforcement decision.

This document sets community expectations; it does not replace applicable law or platform policies.
