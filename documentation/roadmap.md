# Proposed Roadmap

This roadmap is a research plan, not a claim that the listed work is complete. Priorities may change as evidence and contributors become available.

## Phase 0 — Establish project foundations

- Publish project purpose, contribution rules, safety boundaries, and evidence methodology.
- Decide licensing after reviewing intended reuse and contributor rights.
- Set up issue templates and a public list of open questions.

**Exit criteria:** contributors can understand the scope, how to submit evidence, and how claims are reviewed.

## Phase 1 — Map the Nigerian ecosystem

- Identify P&O and rehabilitation institutions, research groups, workshops, manufacturers, suppliers, and relevant standards or regulatory bodies.
- Record capabilities with source links and evidence labels.
- Separate advertised services from independently verified production capabilities.

**Exit criteria:** a sourced, dated ecosystem map with explicit gaps and uncertainty.

## Phase 2 — Materials and process capability

- Investigate relevant polymers, metals, textiles, composites, fasteners, machining, additive manufacturing, moulding, finishing, and repair processes.
- Record material specifications and whether sourcing or processing is domestic.
- Compare cost, quality, repeatability, supply continuity, and maintenance implications.

**Exit criteria:** a component/process map that does not confuse availability with suitability.

## Phase 3 — Component localization case study

- Define a representative EMG-controlled upper-limb prosthesis architecture.
- Break it down into mechanical structure, actuation, transmission, sensors, EMG acquisition, control electronics, firmware, battery and charging, enclosure, user interface, and fitting.
- Assign each component a localization status, evidence source, cost drivers, failure risks, and next validation step.

**Exit criteria:** a reviewed localization matrix and ranked research questions.

## Phase 4 — Simulation and bench research

Only after the architecture and safety boundaries are reviewed:

- Develop simulations or non-human bench demonstrations.
- Document requirements, assumptions, test setup, results, failures, and limitations.
- Prefer low-risk tests before integrated hardware.

**Exit criteria:** reproducible test notes and a clear distinction between simulated, bench-tested, and untested claims.

## Phase 5 — Partnerships and translation

- Seek collaboration with qualified P&O professionals, engineering departments, manufacturers, and relevant regulatory or standards experts.
- Identify training, tooling, quality-management, and funding gaps.
- Consider human factors, fitting, maintenance, repair, and end-user input through appropriate ethical processes.

**Exit criteria:** a justified plan for any next-stage prototype or translational work, with ownership, oversight, funding, and safety responsibilities defined.

## Near-term priority

Complete Phase 0 and begin Phase 1. Do not skip evidence gathering in order to announce a prototype prematurely.
