# BionicsNG Project Charter

## Mission

Investigate, document, and help develop realistic pathways for Nigeria to progressively localize the design, fabrication, manufacture, maintenance, and supply of prosthetic and bionic technologies.

## Problem statement

Prosthetic and bionic systems combine mechanical structures, materials, electronics, sensing, firmware, control algorithms, fitting, clinical expertise, and maintenance. A device assembled locally may still rely heavily on imported components. BionicsNG will examine these layers separately and identify where local capability is demonstrated, where development may be feasible, and where international supply or partnerships remain necessary.

## Initial scope

1. Map the Nigerian prosthetics and orthotics, rehabilitation, university, research, and manufacturing ecosystem.
2. Build an evidence-backed map of materials and manufacturing processes relevant to prosthetic devices.
3. Select a representative EMG-controlled upper-limb prosthesis as an analytical case study.
4. Decompose the case study into mechanical, electrical, sensing, software, power, and user-interface subsystems.
5. Assess localization options, cost drivers, quality requirements, maintenance, and unresolved dependencies.
6. Publish transparent research and, later, appropriately scoped simulation or bench-test work.

## Out of scope for the initial phase

- Claiming that all components can currently be made in Nigeria.
- Clinical efficacy claims or unsupervised patient testing.
- Manufacturing or distributing a medical device for clinical use without the required quality, regulatory, and professional processes.
- Collecting identifiable patient data as a default research method.
- Treating a prototype demonstration as proof of safety, durability, or clinical usefulness.

## Definitions

- **Local design:** design work is performed locally; components may be imported.
- **Local assembly:** imported and/or local components are assembled locally.
- **Local fabrication:** a part is shaped or produced locally from purchased stock or feedstock.
- **Domestic component manufacture:** a component is manufactured domestically, with its upstream supply chain documented separately where possible.
- **Domestic raw-material processing:** a material is processed from a locally sourced feedstock; the feedstock origin and processing chain must be evidenced.
- **Localization:** a graded, component-by-component assessment—not a binary “made locally” label.

## Principles

Evidence-led research; transparent uncertainty; affordability across the lifecycle; repairability; safety; respectful collaboration; attribution; and open discussion.

## Success measures

Early success means publishing a reliable ecosystem map, an auditable evidence register, a component localization matrix, a prioritized list of research gaps, and a transparent process for contributions. It does not require an immediate working bionic device.

## Governance

Maintainers coordinate repository scope, review contributions, and document major decisions publicly where practical. Contributors should disclose relevant conflicts of interest. Technical and clinical claims require appropriate evidence and review; no single contributor's opinion should be represented as a consensus without a documented process.
