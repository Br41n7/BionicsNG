# Component Localization Matrix

**Status:** Initial framework. No localization level should be assigned without evidence.

## Suggested localization levels

- **L0 — Unknown:** no adequate evidence collected.
- **L1 — Local design:** design or engineering work is performed locally; production may be imported.
- **L2 — Local assembly:** components are assembled locally; origins of major parts should be listed.
- **L3 — Local fabrication:** parts are fabricated locally from purchased stock/feedstock.
- **L4 — Domestic component manufacture:** a component is manufactured domestically, with process and quality evidence.
- **L5 — Domestic raw-material processing:** raw material is processed domestically from an identified feedstock; upstream origin must be stated.

These levels are a project research aid, not an official standard. A product may have different levels across different components.

## Initial subsystem checklist

| Subsystem | Questions to investigate |
|---|---|
| Mechanical frame and links | Can geometry, material, machining, finishing, and load testing be sourced locally? |
| Socket, liner, and interface | What clinical expertise, fitting process, material evidence, and user feedback are required? |
| Actuator and transmission | Are motors, gearboxes, tendons, bearings, and drivers locally available or manufactured? |
| EMG electrodes and acquisition | What is imported, what can be designed locally, and what signal-quality validation is needed? |
| Embedded controller and PCB | Is the board locally designed, assembled, or populated from imported components? |
| Firmware and control | What can be developed and tested locally? What safety constraints are required? |
| Battery and power management | What is the origin of cells and protection circuitry? What charging and thermal tests are needed? |
| Enclosure, straps, and covers | Which materials and processes are locally available and suitable? |
| Calibration and test equipment | What measurement capability and traceability exist? |
| Maintenance and spare parts | Can the system be repaired and supported over its expected life? |
