# Materials Map

**Status:** Research template.

Investigate materials that may be relevant to sockets, orthotic structures, prosthetic frames, covers, straps, liners, and mechanical parts. Do not recommend a material for patient contact or load-bearing use without appropriate engineering and biocompatibility evidence.

## Candidate categories

- Thermoplastics and engineering polymers.
- Aluminium and other engineering metals.
- Stainless steels and other corrosion-resistant alloys.
- Elastomers, foams, liners, and textiles.
- Fibre-reinforced composites.
- Adhesives, coatings, fasteners, and surface finishes.
