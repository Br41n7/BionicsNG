# Manufacturing Capabilities

**Status:** Research template.

Map the processes needed for prosthetic and bionic systems without assuming that a process exists locally at the required quality, scale, or cost.

## Process categories to investigate

- CAD and mechanical design.
- CNC machining, turning, milling, sheet-metal work, and welding.
- Polymer machining, moulding, thermoforming, and 3D printing.
- Textile, straps, liners, and soft-goods production.
- Composite lay-up and finishing.
- PCB design and assembly, soldering, enclosure production, and wiring harnesses.
- Sensors, motors, gearboxes, batteries, connectors, and electronic component sourcing.
- Calibration, metrology, fatigue testing, electrical safety testing, and quality management.
- Repair, fitting, adjustment, and replacement-part supply.

## Capability record

For each facility or supplier, record location, process, material range, machine/equipment evidence, tolerance or quality information if available, batch size, lead time, indicative cost and date, certifications if verified, maintenance capacity, source, and limitations.

A machine's presence does not prove that it can reliably produce a particular medical-device component. Verify process capability and quality requirements for the intended component.
