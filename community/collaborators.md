# Collaborators

BionicsNG welcomes contributors in prosthetics and orthotics, rehabilitation, mechanical and materials engineering, electronics, embedded systems, software, robotics, manufacturing, quality systems, and research.

## How to get involved

- Open an issue describing a research question or concrete contribution.
- Submit a source-backed correction or evidence record.
- Propose a focused project with objectives, methods, deliverables, risks, and required expertise.
- Use pull requests for reviewable changes.

## Collaboration record

Participation should be opt-in. Do not add a person's name, affiliation, contact details, biography, or endorsement without their consent. A contributor list should reflect actual contributions and agreed attribution.

For now, use the repository's GitHub Issues and Pull Requests as the public collaboration channels.
