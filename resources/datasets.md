# Datasets

Prefer public, appropriately licensed datasets or synthetic data for early work.

For each dataset record title, creator, source URL, license, version/date, fields and units, collection method, consent or ethical notes where applicable, known biases, restrictions, and intended use.

Do not commit identifiable health data, patient records, or data with unclear permissions. Before using EMG or other human-derived signals, verify license terms and any relevant consent, ethics, and privacy constraints.
