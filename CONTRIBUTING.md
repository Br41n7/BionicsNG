# Contributing to BionicsNG

Thank you for considering a contribution. BionicsNG values verifiable research, practical engineering, clear uncertainty, and respectful collaboration.

## Ways to contribute

- Find and verify Nigerian research groups, clinics, workshops, suppliers, manufacturers, and relevant programmes.
- Review papers, standards, patents, and publicly available technical reports.
- Document material properties, fabrication processes, costs, availability, repairability, and supply risks.
- Improve component breakdowns, localization matrices, CAD, simulations, embedded software, or test plans.
- Correct factual errors, improve documentation, and translate material where useful.

## Before starting

1. Read `README.md`, `documentation/research-methodology.md`, and `documentation/safety-and-ethics.md`.
2. Search existing issues and pull requests.
3. For a new research direction or substantial design, open an issue first. Explain the question, intended output, method, and likely risks.
4. Do not begin work that depends on private, confidential, stolen, or unlawfully obtained data.

## Research contribution requirements

Where applicable, include:

- Claim or research question.
- Source title, author or organization, publication date, and stable URL or DOI.
- Date accessed for web sources.
- Relevant quotation or concise paraphrase and the exact claim it supports.
- Method used, including assumptions and units.
- Geographic and technical scope.
- Limitations, conflicts of interest, and unanswered questions.
- Evidence classification: **Verified, Reported, Hypothesis, or Unknown**.

A supplier's marketing page can establish what the supplier claims, but does not by itself prove local manufacture, performance, quality certification, or production capacity. Distinguish design, assembly, fabrication, component manufacture, and raw-material processing.

Never fabricate citations, prices, capabilities, test results, or conversations. If information cannot be confirmed, label it as unverified or unknown.

## Pull request workflow

1. Fork the repository and create a focused branch.
2. Make a small, reviewable change.
3. Keep unrelated changes out of the same pull request.
4. Check spelling, links, units, source dates, and evidence labels.
5. Explain the purpose, files changed, evidence added, limitations, and any safety implications.
6. Submit the pull request and respond constructively to review.

A contribution may be revised, deferred, or declined if evidence is insufficient, licensing is unclear, safety risks are unresolved, or the work is outside project scope. Review is not a judgment of the contributor's worth.

## Engineering and hardware contributions

- Label designs as conceptual, simulated, bench-tested, or otherwise validated—state the actual test performed.
- Include units, tolerances where known, material specifications, assumptions, and version information.
- Identify failure modes and foreseeable misuse.
- Do not describe a prototype as patient-ready based solely on successful movement or a short demonstration.
- Do not conduct human or patient testing under this project's name without appropriate approvals, professional oversight, risk controls, and applicable regulatory compliance.

## Data, privacy, and intellectual property

Only submit material you have the right to share. Do not commit credentials, API keys, private correspondence without permission, patient records, identifiable health information, or confidential supplier documents. Cite third-party work and respect its license. Ask before including content where permission is unclear.

## Discussion standards

Be specific, evidence-led, and respectful. Criticize claims and methods, not people. Disclose relevant affiliations or financial interests. See `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## Current licensing status

The project has not yet adopted a repository-wide license. Before contributing substantial code, CAD, or other reusable assets, discuss licensing in an issue. Do not submit material that you cannot legally share under a license the project may adopt.
